Why E-Waste Matters

Don't Let Old Electronics Become Tomorrow's Waste

Electronic devices contain valuable materials as well as components that can harm the environment if they are not handled properly. Responsible collection and recycling helps recover resources and reduces waste.

  • ✓ Collect unused and obsolete electronic equipment.
  • ✓ Keep e-waste separate from regular waste.
  • ✓ Identify recyclable electronic materials.
  • ✓ Send e-waste through responsible disposal and recycling channels.
  • ✓ Maintain proper records of collected e-waste.
Campaign Journey

From Collection to Recycling

A simple process can make e-waste management more effective and accountable.

01

Identify

Identify obsolete, unused or damaged electronic equipment that is ready for disposal.

02

Collect

Bring identified e-waste to the designated collection point or campaign activity.

03

Segregate

Separate electronic waste from other waste streams for safe handling and processing.

04

Record

Maintain proper records of collected material, quantity and disposal activities.

05

Recycle

Ensure e-waste reaches appropriate recycling or disposal channels.
